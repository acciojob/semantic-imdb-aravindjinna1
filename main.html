Summary

Interstellar follows Cooper, a former NASA pilot and engineer, who joins a team of astronauts on a dangerous mission through a wormhole near Saturn. Their goal is to find a new habitable planet for humanity as Earth becomes increasingly difficult to live on. The movie explores space travel, time dilation, love, sacrifice, and humanity's survival.
